# Assets the kinetic reel builder expects (not included)

These are third-party or brand files with their own licences, so they are not in the repo. Add your own:

- `fonts/` — the display and caption fonts your themes use (OFL fonts such as Archivo, Anton, Inter work well).
- `emoji/` — 3D emoji images for the "real stickers" (use a set whose licence allows commercial use).
- `sfx/` — short whooshes, pops and hits (licensed for commercial use).
- `voices/` — reference clips / presets for your TTS voices.
- `brands/<theme>/` — per-brand theme: colours, logo, music beds, board image (see `studio/config.py` → `BRANDS`).
